Hatch, new ideas

Today

The doorway arch and the dot. The Stage and the Designer both carry this arch, so a new Hatch mark would move into the lens and the blueprint too.

H1 Split egg recommended

A flat egg opening along a zigzag, warm light from the crack. The name, said plainly, without the realistic shell of the earlier try.

H2 Hatching

Hatching as in drawing: a ball made of parallel pen strokes, one of them orange. A design tool's pun on the name.

H3 Hash

The # of a ticket number, with the last bar orange. Tickets are what Hatch is made of.

H4 Open hatch

A round hatch swung open, light inside: the door kind of hatch.

H5 Sunrise

The orange dot rising over a blue horizon: something new coming up.

H6 Five steps

Hatch's five phases as steps up, the last one orange: a question becomes something shipped. Reads as signal bars, though.
