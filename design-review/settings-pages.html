Settings, every page

The Agents page set the grammar: one grouped Form per page, a row holds a label and one value, pickers are Apple's with the round chevron, actions sit in section headers or under a list, explanations are one footer line, details open in a sheet. Below, every page in that grammar: the sidebar first, GitHub redesigned with much more to see, the existing pages redone, and three new pages. The questions at the bottom decide which ones we build.

The sidebar

Grouped like System Settings, so it can grow: Hatch (how the app behaves), Connections (services), This Mac (programs and files). Project settings stay in the main window, next to the project.

GitHub: much more to see

Today: an account line and a block of repositories per project. Proposed: who you are, what Hatch may do and what is missing (with the fix one click away), how sync is doing, and every repository Hatch uses with its role and state. Tickets repositories get their own section, with the default and which projects share each.
