Components Designer, round two

The Designer is where people choose how their app looks, so it has to be easy to read, easy to move around in, and easy to compare looks in. This page covers every UI and UX decision for it: the words, how you start from a template, the window, how you see and compare options, how you change one look or many at once, how questions are asked, the Matrix, Apple's guidance, settings, rules and foundations, and navigation.

What you said, and what I found

What you said

  • It's cluttered, and it's hard to change something and see what it looks like.
  • No way to give every button in Sheet footer one style, or to use a template for all buttons (right-click Buttons).
  • The inspector is crowded. Look lets you change everything but doesn't say what you should change. ‹ › feels broken.
  • Should you change all buttons at once, or open Bottom bar and change only that?
  • "Against Apple's guidance" is useful but could look nicer.
  • Dark mode doesn't go back to light.
  • The Matrix lists what is decided but gives no overview; it should show previews.
  • Today vs Draft belongs inside editing one role.
  • The bottom bar's options don't change what's drawn; only the inspector does. There should be one place to decide.
  • "Make it a setting" means nothing to users and should come with a recommendation (for buttons, never).

What I found by looking at every page

  • Every ‹ › click makes a git commit in the notebook (through Hatch's API), which is why switching lags.
  • 13 of the 39 look settings have values that draw exactly the same as another value (measured pixel by pixel below). Picking one of those changes nothing visible, which also feels broken.
  • Recommendations only count uses: for toggles Hatch recommends a checkbox even though Hatch's own Glass look and the macOS 27 reference are a mini switch. For "Action with a choice", the Glass look isn't offered at all.
  • Seven roles have generated names, such as "Button (main, list row, card, popover, form)" and menu.toolbar, which is also used in rows, cards and the inspector.
  • A template is chosen by name only, from a menu on Hatch's Components page, before you have seen it. After that the Designer never mentions templates again.
  • The same role inspector and the same rules editor exist twice: on Hatch's Components page and in the Designer.

One look for many CD24–CD26

"Make every button in Sheet footer the same", "use a template for all buttons", "make the inspector all small controls". These are batch changes. They need clear scopes and an honest preview of everything they touch, because a role reaches more than one place.

All buttons, or only Bottom bar?

A place is where a control sits (Bottom bar). A role is one job (Main action). A look belongs to a role, and a role sits in several places: in Hatch, Main action is in Bottom bar, Empty state, Page, Action row and Floating. Changing Main action changes all five, which is the point of a design system. To make it different only in Bottom bar, you make a variant (Main action, Bottom bar) with a reason. The proposal makes this visible: the role view shows every place a change reaches before you keep it, and "Only here…" is always next to it.
