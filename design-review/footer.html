The footer

The footer is always in view, so it can tell you at a glance that everything is calm, or that something needs you. One rule for all of it: quiet when calm, specific when not. Each element is a glyph or a few words; hovering shows a glass card with the details; clicking goes where you act on it. Below: the elements, four complete footers, then the pause control.

The pause control

The pause bars sit next to the circles like a media control, which is not what they do. Alternatives:

A · RECOMMENDED: IN THE SLOTS' MENU
Pause Agents
Open Agents
Agents at Once3 ▸

Right-click (or click empty space) on the circles opens a small menu. No extra icon. While paused, the empty circles turn dashed and the activity line says "Agents paused", so it is never forgotten.

B · IN THE AGENT CARD

A "Pause new agents" switch in the header of every agent card and on the Agents page. Out of sight until you hover.

C · A WORD WHEN PAUSED
Paused

Nothing shows while running; "Paused" with Resume appears only while paused. Combines with A.
